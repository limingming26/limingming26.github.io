About

I am a PhD candidate at the Institute for Sociology, University of Innsbruck. My dissertation, supervised by Prof. Wilfred Uunk, examines economic resources and the gender gap in STEM preferences and choices.

My work connects sociology, economics, education, and labor market research. I use quantitative methods to study gender inequality, STEM education, social stratification, gig and platform work, wage gaps, and China's labor market.

Current Work

  • Major contributor, FWF project Affluence and the Gender Gap in STEM Study Choices (P36789).
