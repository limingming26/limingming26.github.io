EPoS Young Scholar Research Funding (2026) · University of Innsbruck

Gendered Platform Work in China: Stigma, Algorithms, and Household Inequality

Principal investigator
Mingming Li
Funding period
06/2026–06/2027
Status
Current

This project examines how women in platform and low-protection employment navigate occupational stigma, platform rules, and the relationship between paid work and family responsibilities.

The research plan includes a second wave of approximately 30 semi-structured interviews, including follow-up interviews. It compares experiences across family situations and work histories.

EPoS Young Scholar Research Funding (2024) · University of Innsbruck

The Role of the Gig Economy in Gender Wage Gaps in China

Principal investigator
Mingming Li
Funding period
06/2024–06/2025
Status
Completed

This project examined digitalization, entry into gig work, and gender wage inequality in China. Its mixed-method design combined labor-market survey analysis with interviews about working conditions, digital literacy, and family responsibilities.

Period
